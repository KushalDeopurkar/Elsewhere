# Elsewhere

**One-liner:** every kind of event happening in your city (workshops, hackathons, talks, matches, gigs, meetups, exhibitions, volunteering), today, this week and this weekend, gathered from across the web and laid out like a timetable you can act on.

**Audience:** people in their twenties and thirties in Indian metros (and, since the global expansion, any big city) deciding on a phone, around 6pm, where to go: a gig, a workshop, a match, a meetup, a free walk.

**Three jobs**
1. Tell me what is on near me, soonest first, with the time, the place and the price I need to decide.
2. Let me narrow it (when, what kind, free or paid, in plain words) and see it on a map.
3. Keep what I picked: save it, get a reminder, add it to my calendar, bring friends.

**Signature moment:** the home page leads with Ask Elsewhere (plain-words search) beside the city's departures timetable, with a live NOW line between what has started and what departs next. The whole site follows the sun in that city: printed timetable by day, lit departure board after 18:00.

**Out of scope for the redesign:** the backend, scrapers, data model, auth flows and API contracts. Features stay; only their presentation changes.
